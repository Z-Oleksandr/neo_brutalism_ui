## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- Theme: `NeoThemeData` with color, typography, spacing, radius, border,
  shadow and motion tokens; light and dark palettes; `ThemeExtension`
  integration and scoped `NeoTheme`.
- Primitives: `NeoSurface`, `NeoPressable`, `NeoPressableSurface`.
- Components: buttons, inputs, selection controls, navigation, overlays,
  feedback, display, media and date/time pickers.
